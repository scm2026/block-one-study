Block Two

Five more cases, two of them Kellogg\'s, and the first block to carry the casebooks\' own exhibits rather than redrawings of them. The original page is shown as printed; our annotations sit on top of it, numbered, and every added chart is marked as ours.

Source — the casebook's own words, numbers and exhibits Restructured — same content, our arrangement Ours — annotations and charts the casebook doesn't contain

Page tint

Every step is structured the same way: what you have, what to ask for and why — tagged clarify, unlock or test — what you still won't have, then the chain of arithmetic or the chain of reasoning. Source and notes are split into labelled rows rather than prose. "What you have" is only ever what the case hands over unprompted — an unlock question that pays off shows what it reveals directly underneath it, so a fact you'd have to ask for never looks like one you were just given.
